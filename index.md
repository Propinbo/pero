# Pero

Pero is a personal handwriting notes app for Android tablets with an S Pen, written for one student's lectures and seminars. It is not published in any app store.

Pero keeps notes on the tablet. When its owner connects Google Drive, Pero backs the notes up to a `Pero` folder in that owner's own Google Drive, with a PDF of each note for reading on a phone.
