# Pero privacy policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

**What Pero handles.** Pero stores the notes you write (handwriting, photos you add, note and subject names) on your tablet.

**Google Drive.** If you connect Google Drive, Pero asks only for the `drive.file` permission: access to the files Pero itself creates in your Drive. Pero uses it to upload backups of your notes and PDFs of them to a `Pero` folder in your Drive, to keep earlier versions of those files, to read them back when you restore your notes, and to move them to Drive's trash when you delete notes for good. Pero cannot see any other file in your Drive.

**Nothing else leaves your devices.** Pero has no server, no account of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your notes and Google data are never sent to the developer or to anyone else, and are not sold or shared.

**Your control.** You can disconnect Pero at any time in your Google Account (Security, Third-party connections), and delete the `Pero` folder from your Drive. Uninstalling Pero deletes the notes stored on the tablet.
